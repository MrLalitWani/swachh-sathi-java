Executive Summary

Decentralized Urban Waste Management System

Swachh Sathi is an AI/heuristic-powered civic platform designed to streamline municipal waste handling, citizen engagement, and sanitation worker logistics. By pairing real-time citizen grievance reporting with geospatial vicinity sensitivity weighting (proximity to hospitals, schools, parks, and residential zones) and route optimization, municipal corporations achieve faster turnaround times and dignified working conditions for sanitation personnel.

Pillar 1: Citizen Empowerment & Source Segregation

  • Transparent Complaint Lifecycle: Citizens receive real-time updates as their complaint moves from Reported → Assigned → Pending Verification → Resolved.
  • Education & Segregation at Source: Embedded guides on segregating wet, dry, hazardous, and sanitary waste, with home composting techniques.
  • Civic Inclusion: Direct pathway for unorganized waste pickers and citizens to register and apply for formal municipal employment.

Pillar 2: Sanitation Worker Dignity & Safety

  • Dynamic Route Optimization: Nearest-neighbor travel sequencing algorithm reduces worker transit time, fuel consumption, and vehicle wear-and-tear.
  • Verification with Proof: Cleaned spot photos uploaded directly through mobile devices ensure fair recognition and dispute prevention.
  • Mandatory Safety & PPE Integration: Automated alerts for hazardous and chemical waste spots with recommended protective gear.

Pillar 3: Geospatial Vicinity Priority Engine

Rather than treating all garbage reports with first-come-first-serve priority, Swachh Sathi computes a dynamic urgency index using the Haversine distance formula against municipal Sensitive Facility POIs within a 500-meter radius:

Schools

Weight: 5.0 (Critical)

Hospitals

Weight: 4.5 (Critical)

Public Parks

Weight: 3.5 (High)

Residential

Weight: 3.0 (Medium)

Commercial

Weight: 2.0 (Low)

Architecture & Technical Implementation

Backend: Java 21, Spring Boot 3.4, Spring Security, JWT (stateless), Spring Data JPA, Hibernate, OpenAPI (Swagger UI).
Database: H2 In-Memory for development and zero-config deployment; PostgreSQL & PostGIS ready for production scale.
Mapping: OpenStreetMap + Leaflet JS for cost-free, API-key-independent interactive map rendering and route visualization.
Localization: Trilingual interface engine supporting English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Marathi (मराठी).
