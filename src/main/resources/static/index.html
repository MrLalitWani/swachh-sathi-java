Waste Segregation Guide

🟢 Wet / Organic

Food scraps, vegetable peels, leaves. Suitable for composting.

🔵 Dry / Recyclable

Paper, plastic bottles, metal cans, glass jars.

🔴 Hazardous / E-Waste

Batteries, paints, chemicals, CFL bulbs, electronics.

🟡 Sanitary Waste

Wrap securely in newspaper with a red mark.
